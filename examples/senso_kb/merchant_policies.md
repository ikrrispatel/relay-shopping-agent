# RELAY synthetic retailer knowledge base: merchant policies

This is fictional test data for the RELAY demo. It is not a claim about a real merchant.

VoltEdge sells the FastCharge USB-C Laptop Charger (T005). ChargeCraft sells the Compact USB-C Laptop Charger (T006). Both merchants support USB-C laptop charging for compatible USB-C power-delivery devices. The catalog's stock and price fields are the dynamic source of truth and must be revalidated immediately before checkout.

Synthetic fulfillment rule: the catalog's `delivery` tag is a product attribute used for ranking only. It is not a live delivery guarantee. Returns are accepted within 30 synthetic days for unused items; refunds are issued after the returned item is received.
